**Case ID: E**

1. **Addition (Sum of Durations):**
   - 10 + 20 = 30  
   - 30 + 15 = 45  
   - 45 + 25 = 70  
   - 70 + 20 = **110**

2. **Actual Sum:** 110

3. **Claim Correctness:** The actual sum (110) does not equal the claimed total (100). Therefore, the claim is **incorrect**.

4. **Budget Compliance:** The budget is 120. Since the actual sum (110) is less than the budget (120), the claim complies with the budget. Remainder = Budget - Actual Sum = 120 - 110 = **10** (positive remainder indicates under budget).

5. **Signed Remainder:** +10

Summary:
- Addition: 110
- Claim Correctness: Incorrect
- Budget Compliance: Under budget, Remainder = 10
