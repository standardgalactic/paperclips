Write a 550–750 word argumentative essay titled The Cost of Constant Renovation. Use only the following fictional observations as evidence: Building A replaced intact lobby tiles twice in five years. Residents reported daytime drilling on 18 of 30 observation days. Building B repaired damaged tiles individually and recorded drilling on 4 of 30 observation days. Neither building measured structural safety, property value, or long-term maintenance costs. Argue for a defensible maintenance policy. Distinguish observations from interpretations, discuss at least one alternative explanation, and acknowledge what the comparison cannot establish. Use connected prose with no bullet points. Do not invent studies, quotations, statistics, or citations.
