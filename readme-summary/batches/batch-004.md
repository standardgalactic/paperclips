# Batch 4

**Technical Requirements**

- Required LaTeX packages include `fontspec`, `unicode-math`, `geometry`, `fancyhdr`, `microtype`, `setspace`, `ragged2e`, `calc`, `multicol`, `paracol`, `marginnote`, `changepage`, and `atbegshi`. Installation instructions for Ubuntu/WSL are provided.
- Pagination is dual‑countered: Western numerals appear in the left margin (English side) while Eastern Arabic numerals run on the right margin (Arabic side). This reflects a reading direction from outside to inside.

**Narrative Structure & Typographic Phases**

The manuscript follows an evolving typographic phase map:

1. **Stable Phase (Chapters I‑II)** – Nova Mono dominant; occasional SGA anomaly.
2. **Bifurcating Phase (III‑IV)** – Cursive Galactic introduced; Arabic becomes a non‑translated layer; spatial fragmentation begins.
3. **Propagating Phase (V‑VII)** – SGA escapes into connective tissue; repetition with variation; drip desynchronizes.
4. **Retroactive Phase (VIII)** – Typography reassigns necessity to the past without glyph change.
5. **Midpoint Phase (IX)** – Non‑commuting columns and reading order alter meaning; no privileged path.
6. **Fractured Phase (X‑XI)** – Clypto font appears unannounced; character identity becomes non‑unified; cognitive ontology destabilized.
7. **Drift Phase (XII‑XIV)** – “Byobu Drift Cycle” activates: right pages split, Arabic counter advances twice per physical page, creating instability and fire exposure effects.
8. **Return Phase (XV‑XVI)** – Byobu pagination reverses; sparse equilibrium is restored after a single irreversible transformation.
9. **Transmission Phase (XVII‑XXI)** – Fonts degrade as if copied; approximate replication occurs; dispersal of textual fragments.
10. **Normalized Incoherence (XXII)** – Anomalies become permanent; the system appears stable only because incoherence is its baseline.

**Macro & Stub Details**

- Macros such as `\sga{text}`, `\cga{text}`, `\logico{text}`, and others are defined to render specific typographic styles.
- Special macros like `\rightpagesplit` and `\leftpagecatch` manage the Byobu pagination drift, stepping Arabic counters accordingly.
- Stub chapters compile cleanly; they contain annotations for screenplay coverage, structural operations, and placeholder text visible in the final PDF.
