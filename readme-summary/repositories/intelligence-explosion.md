# intelligence-explosion

The repository “intelligence-explosion” aims to explore and develop methodologies related to rapid cognitive enhancement, often referred to as an "intelligence explosion." It contains research on artificial general intelligence (AGI) models, heuristic frameworks for accelerating learning processes, and experimental software simulating self-improving systems. Key components include:

- **Conceptual Models**: Papers outlining theoretical constructs such as the “recursive self-improvement loop” and “capability amplification.”
- **Software Implementations**: Codebases demonstrating prototype AGI agents capable of iterative refinement through feedback loops.
- **Terminology**: Uses terms like “intelligence scaling,” “meta-learning algorithms,” and “bounded rationality hacks.”
- **Frameworks**: Incorporates formal mathematical models, including differential equations for growth dynamics and probabilistic programming for uncertainty management in self-improvement processes.
- **Relationships**: Explicitly linked to projects like “AGI-safety” and “neuro-symbolic AI,” aiming to integrate safety protocols with rapid intelligence gain research.

The implementation is currently at an experimental stage, with many components still under active development and lacking comprehensive testing or validation. The README emphasizes ongoing verification processes and cautions about the speculative nature of some claims due to limited empirical evidence.

KEYWORDS:
intelligence explosion, artificial general intelligence, self-improvement loop, recursive algorithms, AGI safety, neuro-symbolic AI, experimental software, rapid cognition models, mathematical frameworks, feedback loops.
