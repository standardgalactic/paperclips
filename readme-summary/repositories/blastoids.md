# blastoids

**Summary**

*Blastoids Pic Breeder* is an interactive browser‑based platform for exploring and evolving images through user interaction. It constructs a **Preference Field (Φ)**—a unit vector in CLIP embedding space—that continuously evolves as users rate items (“Awesome”, “OK”, “Horrible”). This field defines the direction of alignment, influencing which images are surfaced, reinforced, or used to generate new ones.

The system operates on a **dual‑space architecture**: *Pixel Space* for visual rendering and *Embedding Space* for similarity/comparison. Mutation operators (crop, blend, color shift) act primarily in pixel space but also span embedding space via the blend operator, preserving coherence between perception and evaluation.

**Breeding Layer:** New images are generated by interpolating latent vectors of selected parents and adding controlled noise, then normalizing and applying a pixel‑space transformation. Early generations favor local refinement (e.g., cropping), while later generations promote compositional blending across embedding space, creating an evolutionary trajectory from structure to synthesis.

**Sampling & Non‑Collapse:** Image selection uses a softmax distribution over composite scores that combine alignment with the Preference Field, explicit ratings, novelty terms, and decay penalties. A temperature floor ensures all items retain nonzero probability of selection, preventing deterministic collapse. Sampling draws from two pools: the original corpus and generated offspring, guaranteeing continual exploration.

**Persistence & Decay:** Rated offspring persist indefinitely, while un‑rated offspring decay over generations and are pruned. This selective memory maintains continuity with foundational structure while responding to ongoing interaction.

Theoretically, the system aligns with RSVP (dynamic constraint), TARTAN (recursive generation under historical constraints), and Chain of Memory (conditional persistence). Mathematically, it enforces invariants such as normalized latent vectors and a strictly positive temperature floor for stability.

**Key Claims:** The platform demonstrates weak autopoiesis—continuous regeneration of its aesthetic ecology without becoming static or chaotic—through the interplay of perception, evaluation, and generation driven by user‑defined preference fields.
