# animal-social

Summary:

The Animal Social repository is part of a larger project titled "Your Mind as a Dynamic Landscape," which explores the intersection of social behavior in animals and cognitive frameworks. It references several key papers and drafts, including:

1. **Reachability**: A document on reachability concepts likely related to modeling interactions within animal societies.
2. **Incompleteness, Null Signals, and the Cosmological Plenum**: Explores philosophical and cosmological ideas about gaps in knowledge (incompleteness) and their relation to social signaling mechanisms.
3. **From Catharsis to Geometric Bayesianism**: Connects emotional release theories with geometric Bayesian models for understanding decision-making processes in animal groups.
4. **Critique of Catharsis Model**: Provides a critical analysis of traditional catharsis models applied to animal behavior, questioning their validity.
5. **Pneumatic Theory of Emotions**: Introduces a novel theory linking emotions to pneumatic (air-filled) systems, potentially offering new insights into emotional expression in animals.

The repository appears to be in an experimental stage, as indicated by the presence of drafts and references to ongoing research rather than fully mature implementations. It focuses on theoretical frameworks and conceptual explorations rather than ready-to-use software tools.
