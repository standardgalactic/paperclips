# circle-of-fifths

**Purpose:** Interactive visualizer for the Circle of Fifths that enables users to transpose chord progressions by rotating a musical keyboard layout.

**Core Concepts:**
- **Circle of Fifths:** A geometric representation showing the relationships among the 12 tones of the chromatic scale, their corresponding key signatures, and the associated major/minor chords.
- **Ergodic Music Design:** The project’s design philosophy emphasizes interactive exploration of music theory through visual feedback.

**Key Features:**
1. **Outer Circle (Major Chords):** Displays standard diatonic major triads for each note on a 12‑note keyboard.
2. **Inner Circle (Relative Minor Chords):** Shows the corresponding minor chords, allowing immediate identification of key relationships.
3. **Dynamic Transposition:** Buttons let users rotate left or right to shift all displayed chord labels and progressions accordingly.

**Implementation Status:**
- The README indicates a functional prototype (`index.html`) with visual updates for common progressions (e.g., I‑IV‑V) as the circle is rotated.
- No mention of extensive testing, third‑party integrations, or production readiness; it appears to be an experimental proof‑of‑concept.

**Relationships:**
- Directly related to standard music theory concepts such as key signatures and chord inversions.
- Not explicitly linked to other projects beyond the visual demonstration of musical relationships.

**Unusual Claims:**
- The project leverages a “Knot of Fifths” (likely a stylized representation) and a “Piano of Fifths,” suggesting an extended visual metaphor for keyboard layout rather than conventional notation.
