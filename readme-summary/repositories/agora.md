# agora

SUMMARY:

Agora is a research repository exploring the hypothesis that variations in collagen levels within interstitial and integumentary tissues contribute to dimorphic traits—such as size, function, or resilience—in different organisms or sexes. It integrates concepts from biomedical science, evolutionary biology, and philosophical ideas (e.g., "Thick Skin, Hidden Heart" from Chinese philosophy) to propose a comprehensive framework for understanding how these biological differences manifest and their potential applications.

KEYWORDS: Agora repository, collagen hypothesis, dimorphic traits, interstitial tissues, integumentary tissues, biomedical research, evolutionary biology, Thick Skin Hidden Heart philosophy, mechanical properties, genetic regulation.
