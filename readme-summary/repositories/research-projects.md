# research-projects

Summary:

The research-projects repository under the namespace "research-projects" focuses on interdisciplinary explorations spanning theoretical concepts, experimental systems, and software artifacts. It primarily investigates:

1. **Constraint-First Topology** - An essay and audio overview examining a novel approach to organizing complex systems based on constraints rather than traditional hierarchical structures.

2. **Dynamics of the Capital Order** - A research paper and accompanying audio overview that analyze economic models and their implications for capital allocation and market behavior.

3. **Distributed Minds and Generative Substrates** - An essay exploring how cognitive processes might be distributed across multiple agents or systems, with potential applications in artificial intelligence and neuroscience.

4. **Steam-Formed Cellulose Domes** - A technical essay detailing the design and construction of domes using steam-formed cellulose materials, showcasing innovative architectural techniques and material science applications.

The repository contains academic essays and audio overviews that detail methodologies, findings, and visualizations related to these projects. Implementation status varies; some components are in early development stages while others represent more mature research prototypes. Relationships with other projects are not explicitly detailed within the README, suggesting an independent focus on each topic.
