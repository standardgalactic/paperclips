# admissibility-lab

Summary:

The admissibility-lab repository explores the theoretical framework of "Intelligence as Perspectival Convergence," which posits that intelligence emerges from the convergence of multiple perspectives rather than a singular, objective truth. Key concepts include:

- **Perspectival Convergence**: The idea that diverse viewpoints can align to form a coherent understanding.
- **Unbundling Intelligence**: Decomposing intelligence into its constituent parts for analysis.
- **Path Over State**: Focusing on processes (paths) rather than static states in the evolution of intelligent systems.
- **Containers Before Contents**: Emphasizing the importance of the structure or container that holds information, prior to the content itself.
- **Kinetic Containment**: A method for dynamically managing and containing information flows within a system.
- **Copies Before Originals**: Suggesting that copies (or manifestations) can precede and inform the creation of original entities.
- **The Geometry of Convergence**: A mathematical framework describing how different perspectives geometrically converge.

Additional resources include visualizations such as the intelligence-infographic.png, and specialized concepts like:

- **Autobiographical Erasure**: The process of removing or obscuring personal narratives within data to prevent bias.
- **The Admissibility Manifold**: A conceptual space that defines conditions under which information is considered admissible or valid within a given context.

The repository contains notes on the Admissibility Manifold, indicating an ongoing development rather than a fully matured implementation. Relationships with other projects are implied but not explicitly detailed in the README.

KEYWORDS: intelligence convergence, perspectival theory, unbundling, path over state, kinetic containment, copies originals, autobiographical erasure, admissibility manifold, container content, geometric convergence.
