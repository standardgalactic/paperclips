# diatribe

Summary:

Diatribe is a research repository centered around the concept of "Manufactured Necessity," which proposes a counterfactual formalism to analyze structural waste within discretionary infrastructure. It explores several interrelated theories and frameworks, including:

1. **Conditioned Emptiness** - Focuses on realized use as the correct denominator for embodied burden.
2. **The Economy of Replaced Things** - Examines withdrawn admissibility when physical capability persists.
3. **Artificial Scarcity** - Investigates market-preserving destruction and withholding mechanisms.
4. **Needless Miles** - Analyzes displacement that purchases too little additional capability.
5. **Borrowed Climate** - Studies environments maintained against a local reference trajectory.
6. **Merchants of Lack** - Explores advertising and the construction of the reference state.
7. **Disposable Ceremony** - Looks at destruction, consumption, and the recursion of recognition.
8. **Purple People Eaters** - A unique concept likely related to societal or ecological impacts.
9. **Revenue from Weakness** - Proposes corrective taxation addressing fiscal dependence issues.
10. **Contrast as Control** - Introduces proxy teachers and prioritized semantic attention methods.

The repository includes a visual representation (01-system.png) illustrating the system architecture discussed in these papers. All referenced documents are external links to detailed PDFs, indicating that the repository serves primarily as a collection of research artifacts rather than an operational software implementation.

KEYWORDS: diatribe, manufactured necessity, artificial scarcity, disposable ceremony, economic waste analysis, counterfactual formalism, infrastructure studies, environmental impact, fiscal dependence.
