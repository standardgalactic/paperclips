**Tools should enlarge the human continuation set, not merely imitate the human.**

This repository develops a family of arguments about augmentation, imitation, observability, institutional power, continuation, and the allocation of uncertainty. Across the papers, the recurring question is not simply what a system can do, but what becomes reachable or unreachable for people because that system exists.

The project moves from **hyperbionic reading**, where typography and transformation can augment rather than replace human interpretation, outward into a broader theory of technological and institutional power.

A compact statement of the project is:

**capability ≠ continuation**

and its corresponding design criterion is:

**Progress(M, H) ∝ ΔΓ_H(M)**

The relevant measure of a technology is not merely its proximity to human performance, but the change it produces in the set of futures reachable by humans.

## The Papers

### [Hyperbionic Reading](hyperbionic_reading.pdf)

The originating argument.

Rather than asking whether a machine can reproduce human reading, the paper asks how technical systems can enlarge the perceptual and interpretive capacities of the reader.

The governing distinction is:

**Imitation → reproduce an existing human capability**

**Augmentation → enlarge the human capability set**

Hyperbionic reading therefore supplies the positive design principle for the rest of the repository: technology should change what a person can reach, not merely transfer an existing human operation into a machine.

Source: [`hyperbionic_reading.tex`](hyperbionic_reading.tex)


![](02-hyperbionic-reading.png)

### [Evidentiary Decoupling](evidentiary-decoupling.pdf)

A theory of systems whose visible signals remain intact while their relationship to the underlying state deteriorates.

The central problem can be expressed as:

**I(G; S) ↓ while S remains apparently stable**

A signal may continue to look legitimate even as its informational relationship to the state it supposedly represents weakens.

This creates a general warning for audits, benchmarks, institutional metrics, and machine-generated representations:

**persistence of signal ≠ persistence of evidence**

Source: [`evidentiary-decoupling.tex`](evidentiary-decoupling.tex)

---

### [Forgotten Specification](forgotten-specification.pdf)

An account of how contingent design objectives become mistaken for natural constraints.

The transformation is:

**choice → benchmark → investment → infrastructure → apparent inevitability**

or, more compactly:

**O ∈ D → O ∈ K**

An objective **O** begins in the design space **D** but eventually behaves institutionally as though it belonged to the constraint set **K**.

The imitation objective in AI provides the central example. Humanlike generality is not simply discovered as the destination of technological development. It can become the destination because research, benchmarks, capital, infrastructure, and expectations have been organized around making it so.

Source: [`forgotten-specification.tex`](forgotten-specification.tex)

---

### [Allocation of Uncertainty](allocation-of-uncertainty.pdf)

A framework for analyzing claims that a social, environmental, or institutional loss was unavoidable.

To call a loss **necessary** is to make a counterfactual claim about the feasible set:

**∄A′ ∈ F(B): Γ(A′) > Γ(A*)**

If another feasible allocation could have preserved more continuation, the loss was not forced by the constraints. It was selected.

The paper distinguishes:

**Γ_F** — forced outcomes
**Γ_S** — selected outcomes
**Γ_U** — underdetermined outcomes

The institutional objective is not the elimination of uncertainty but **resolvability**:

**Γ_U → Γ_F ∪ Γ_S**

Source: [`allocation-of-uncertainty.tex`](allocation-of-uncertainty.tex)

---

### [Value of Floods](value-of-floods.pdf)

An examination of differential survivability and the political economy of private exit.

The central metaphor is the **Ark and the Flood**. The value of private protection is relational:

**V_ark = E[s_A] − E[s_B]**

If expected survivability outside the protected system declines while survivability inside remains stable, the relative value of the Ark increases.

This creates the possibility of an **Exit Feedback Loop**:

**Q ↓ → V_ark ↑ → private exit ↑ → exposure ↓ → support for Q ↓ → Q ↓**

The paper asks what happens when those with the greatest control over common systems become the least dependent upon their continued functioning.

Source: [`value-of-floods.tex`](value-of-floods.tex)

---

### [Architectures of Impunity](Architectures_of_Impunity.pdf)

The framework brought together at the level of institutional power.

Impunity is not simply the possession of wealth or authority. It emerges from a particular configuration of continuation, control, epistemic capacity, and exposure:

**Γᵢ ↑**
**κᵢ ↑**
**εᵢ ↑**
**eᵢ ↓**

The dangerous actor is therefore not merely powerful, but powerful while increasingly insulated from the consequences of the systems they influence.

The architecture asks four questions simultaneously:

**Who can continue?**

**Who can control?**

**Who can know and establish what counts as knowledge?**

**Who remains exposed to the consequences?**

## The Conceptual Spine

The papers can be read as one continuous argument:

**Hyperbionic Reading**
↓
**augmentation rather than imitation**
↓
**Forgotten Specification**
↓
**chosen objectives become apparent constraints**
↓
**Evidentiary Decoupling**
↓
**representation separates from underlying state**
↓
**Allocation of Uncertainty**
↓
**selected losses acquire the appearance of necessity**
↓
**Value of Floods**
↓
**private continuation gains value as common continuation deteriorates**
↓
**Architectures of Impunity**

The recurring invariant is:

> **The surface is not the mapping.**

A benchmark is not the objective that produced it. A signal is not necessarily evidence. Aggregate capability is not continuation. Compensation is not restored agency. Private survivability is not common resilience. An observed loss is not proof of necessity.

## Core Notation

| Symbol             | Meaning                                                     |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Γᵢ**             | Continuation set of actor *i*: the futures still reachable  |
| **Γ_H(M)**         | Human continuation available in the presence of machine *M* |
| **ΔΓ_H(M)**        | Continuation gain produced by *M*                           |
| **Q**              | Quality of the common substrate                             |
| **eᵢ**             | Exposure of actor *i* to changes in **Q**                   |
| **κᵢ**             | Control capacity                                            |
| **εᵢ**             | Epistemic capacity                                          |
| **εˢ, εᴰ, εᴬ, εᴵ** | Specification, Data, Audit, and Interpretive capacities     |
| **Γ_F**            | Forced region                                               |
| **Γ_S**            | Selected region                                             |
| **Γ_U**            | Underdetermined region                                      |
| **Γ_priv**         | Privately maintained continuation                           |
| **F(B)**           | Feasible allocations under constraint set **B**             |

A compact representation of the social architecture is:

**zᵢ = (Γᵢ, κᵢ, εᵢ, eᵢ)**

The dangerous configuration is not simply inequality in any one coordinate, but their decoupling:

**high continuation + high control + high epistemic authority + low exposure**

## The Larger Question

Across these papers, the object of analysis changes, but the structural question remains remarkably stable.

For technology:

**Does capability enlarge human continuation or replace it?**

For institutions:

**Does representation preserve access to reality or replace it?**

For crisis governance:

**Was the loss forced or selected?**

For political economy:

**Does private protection supplement the commons or become valuable because the commons has failed?**

For epistemology:

**Who controls the transition from event → evidence → accepted account?**

And for power:

**Who retains control after withdrawing exposure?**

The Expensive Future begins when these questions converge: when common continuation contracts, private continuation becomes purchasable, decision-makers become insulated from common failure, and the same institutions capable of selecting losses acquire the epistemic authority to describe those losses as inevitable.

The alternative is not simply better redistribution after the fact.

It is **augmentation, adjacency, observability, resolvability, and repair**.

> **The measure of progress is not how human the machine becomes, but how much more possible the human future becomes.**
